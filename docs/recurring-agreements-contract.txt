========================================================================
Locked decisions
========================================================================

1. Agreement creation and full deposit collection are atomic.
2. Persisted deposit lifecycle starts at HELD (no PENDING).
3. Booking Transaction remains booking-specific; deposits use
   RecurringDepositTransaction.
4. Forfeiture is status/audit only, not a cash transaction.
5. Preview uses previewed_at; cancel recalculates with timezone.now().
6. Settlements include deposit collections/refunds via
   SettlementRecurringDepositTransaction; net =
   booking payments + collections - refunds.
7. Staff refund = assigned court AND original deposit collector.
8. Recurring booking payment cancel does not demote CONFIRMED to HOLD.
9. Occurrence generation uses RECURRING_GENERATION_HORIZON_WEEKS constant.
10. cancellation_effective_date is the first released occurrence.
11. No replace endpoint or replaced_by field. New period = cancel + create.
